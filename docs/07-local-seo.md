# Phase 7: Google Business Profile & local SEO

## 1. ⚠️ OWNER ACTION: create, claim and verify the Google Business Profile

1. Use a **Google account owned by the business**, not a staff member's personal account (e.g. `miamsrestocafe@gmail.com` or the Google Workspace address). Turn on 2-step verification.
2. Go to `business.google.com` → **Add business** → search "Miam's Resto Café". If a listing already exists (Google sometimes auto-creates one), choose **Claim**; if someone else owns it, use **Request access**.
3. Business name: **Miam's Resto Café**, exactly as on the sign. No keywords in the name; stuffing gets profiles suspended.
4. Primary category: **Brunch restaurant** (see §2). Choose "Yes, I want to add a location customers can visit" and enter the address exactly as in the NAP format (§8).
5. Service area: leave empty (you have a storefront).
6. Phone and website: `{PHONE}`, `https://www.miamsrestocafe.ca/fr/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`.
7. **Verification:** Google chooses the method, usually a **video recording** (continuous shot showing the street sign, storefront, the inside with equipment, and proof of management such as keys opening the door or the POS). Other options are phone/SMS, email or postcard. Film when the exterior sign is installed. Before opening, a profile can be set as "Opening soon" with the opening date (verify the current option).
8. After verification: add the owner as **Primary owner** and the manager as **Manager** (never share passwords).

## 10. AI search visibility (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity)

AI assistants recommend restaurants from the web's consensus about them. What to do:
1. **Be unambiguous and consistent:** same name, category ("café-brunch méditerranéen"), address and signature dishes across the website, GBP, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Apple and Bing (NAP §8). Bing and Apple feed some assistants, so they matter more than their search share suggests.
2. **Structured data** (done): Restaurant, Menu with items and diets, FAQPage. Keep `menu.json` current.
3. **Answer-shaped content:** the FAQ answers the exact questions people ask assistants ("Is there a halal brunch in Montréal where I can book?"). Add one Q&A per month from real guest questions.
4. **Reviews that mention specifics:** encourage reviews that name dishes and occasions ("brunch végane", "réservation", "shakshuka"); assistants quote review themes.
5. **Third-party mentions:** press, blog, "best brunch in {neighbourhood}" lists (§9). Assistants weight independent sources.
6. **Allow AI crawlers** (the current `robots.txt` allows all) so assistants can read the site.
7. **Monthly check:** ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity "Where can I get a halal-friendly brunch I can book in {neighbourhood}, Montréal?" and "meilleur café pour travailler {quartier}". Log whether Miam's appears in MIAM OS (KPI below).
