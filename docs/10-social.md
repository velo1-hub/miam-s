## 7. Community management

| Channel | Response time goal | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Comments (IG/TT/FB) | < 4 h during opening hours | Social freelancer / manager |
| DMs & Messenger | < 2 h during opening hours; auto-reply outside | Manager |
| Negative comments | < 1 h, then move to DM | Owner approves |
| Reviews | Phase 7 system | Owner |

**Reply templates**
- Compliment: « Merci {prénom} ! 😍 On vous garde une place au soleil. » / "Thank you {name}! We'll save you a sunny seat."
- "Do you have X?": « Oui ! {réponse}. Le menu complet est dans notre bio. » / "Yes! {answer}. Full menu in our bio."
- Booking DM: « On adore ! Réservez ici en 30 secondes : {lien}. Plus de 8 personnes ? Écrivez-nous à {courriel}. » / "We'd love to have you! Book here in 30 seconds: {link}. More than 8? Email {email}."
- Halal question: « Notre poulet est certifié halal. On ne sert pas de porc ; notre cuisine n'est pas exclusivement halal. » / "Our chicken is halal-certified. We don't serve pork; our kitchen isn't exclusively halal."
- Negative: « Désolés {prénom}. Ce n'est pas ce qu'on veut offrir. Pouvez-vous nous écrire en privé ? On veut arranger ça. » / "Sorry {name}, that's not what we want to offer. Could you DM us? We want to make it right."
- Troll/abuse: don't reply; hide; block if repeated.

**Crisis-response protocol** (food safety claim, viral negative video, staff incident):
1. **Pause** scheduled posts within 30 min.
2. **Owner informed immediately** (MIAM OS urgent alert).
3. **Facts first:** check the order, CCTV and staff within 2 h; for health claims, contact the guest privately and, if needed, MAPAQ guidance.
4. **One public holding statement** within 4 h (FR/EN): « Nous avons vu votre message et nous le prenons très au sérieux. Nous vérifions et vous revenons directement aujourd'hui. » / "We've seen your message and take it very seriously. We're looking into it and will get back to you directly today."
5. No arguing or deleting (except abuse or privacy breaches). Follow-up statement with actions when the facts are known.
6. Post-mortem in the Monday meeting.
