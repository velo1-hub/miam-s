## 2. Positioning

**Three options:**

| | Statement | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The all-day Mediterranean table** ⭐ | For neighbourhood regulars, hybrid workers and brunch lovers in central Montréal who want more than eggs and coffee, **Miam's Resto Café** is the **all-day Mediterranean café-kitchen** that **feeds you like family from the first latte to the last mezze**, because **everything is made in-house from Mediterranean home recipes, the coffee is roasted in Montréal, and you can book, so you never queue for brunch.** | Owns the whole day and the "no queue" pain point; broadest revenue | Needs execution across dayparts |
| B. The Mediterranean brunch | For Montréal brunch fans, Miam's is the brunch spot that brings Mediterranean sunshine to your weekend because of its signature shakshuka and pastries. | Sharp, easy to communicate | Weekday revenue gap; brunch is crowded |
| C. The healthy Mediterranean café | For health-conscious urbanites, Miam's is the Mediterranean café that makes eating well delicious. | Trend-aligned | Undermines generosity and indulgence (brioche, pistachio), and "health" is a crowded, cold territory |

**Recommendation: A.** Only A matches the revenue model (weekday mornings and lunch make up ~60 % of projected sales, see [menu engineering](generated/menu-engineering.md)) and turns the #1 competitor complaint (waiting) into a reason to believe. The brunch identity from B still leads the weekend content.

**Short positioning line (internal):** *Café sérieux, cuisine de famille méditerranéenne, toute la journée.* (Serious coffee, Mediterranean family cooking, all day.)

## 3. Personality & archetype

**Archetype: The Caregiver × The Everyman.** Think of the favourite aunt with a full table: warm, generous, a little cheeky, never precious. The Caregiver gives the generosity; the Everyman keeps it unpretentious and neighbourly.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Encore un peu de pain pour saucer ?" / "More bread for the sauce?" | "Would you care for an additional bread service?" |
| Tell guests what's homemade, with pride and specifics | Use "artisanal", "curated", "elevated" |
| Joke gently ("Oui, les frites sont DANS la pita.") | Sarcasm, irony at the guest's expense |
| Speak the guest's language first | Correct a guest's French or English |
| Say sorry plainly and fix it fast | Hide behind policy ("That's not possible") |
| Use sensory, concrete words: crisp, warm, simmered, golden | Use vague superlatives: amazing, best-ever, unique |

## 4. Customer personas

### Persona 1: **Camille, 34, the hybrid-worker regular**
- **Who:** UX designer, works from home Mon/Fri, lives 6 minutes' walk away. French-speaking, comfortable in English.
- **Needs:** reliable Wi-Fi and outlets, great latte, a lunch that isn't a sad salad, not being judged for staying 2 hours.
- **Habits:** coffee 3×/week, lunch 1×/week, Saturday brunch with her partner twice a month.
- **Occasions:** "work from café" mornings, weekend brunch, office lunch when her team meets.
- **Channels:** Instagram (saves Reels), Google Maps, word of mouth, newsletter if it's useful.
- **Objections:** "Cafés with good food get too loud to work", "$6.50 lattes add up."
- **What wins her:** laptop ledge with outlets, loyalty that rewards frequency (Phase 12), honey-cardamom latte, a Friday "formule midi".

### Persona 2: **Karim & friends, 22–27, the weekend brunch group**
- **Who:** university students and young professionals, groups of 3–6, mixed French/English/Arabic speakers, some eat halal, one is vegan.
- **Needs:** a place that works for everyone's diet, Instagrammable food, generous portions for the price, and no 45-minute queue.
- **Habits:** brunch 2–3×/month, rotate between spots, pick via TikTok and Instagram.
- **Occasions:** weekend brunch, birthdays, post-exam celebrations; Thursday mezze evening.
- **Channels:** TikTok, Instagram Stories, Google "brunch near me", group chats.
- **Objections:** "Brunch is $30 now", "Halal? Vegan? They never have both."
- **What wins them:** online booking for 4–8, halal chicken and vegan dishes clearly labelled, brunch-for-two formula, the shakshuka pan moment for video, a birthday reward.

### Persona 3: **Sophie, 41, the busy parent and office organiser**
- **Who:** HR coordinator at a 60-person company nearby; mother of two (4 and 8). English-speaking, bilingual at work.
- **Needs:** a kid-friendly weekend spot with a menu the kids eat and she enjoys, and a dependable caterer for team mornings and lunches (labelled allergens!).
- **Habits:** family brunch 1–2×/month on Sunday; orders catering 1–2×/month for work.
- **Occasions:** Sunday family brunch, team breakfasts, onboarding days, holiday party.
- **Channels:** Google search ("office catering montreal", "kid friendly brunch"), Facebook, email, colleague recommendations.
- **Objections:** "Caterers are unreliable on time and allergens", "Brunch spots hate strollers."
- **What wins her:** kids' menu and high chairs, allergen-labelled individual catering boxes with invoice and PO support, 48-hour ordering, delivery by 11:45, a corporate account.

## 7. Tone-of-voice guide

**Voice principles**
1. **Chaleureux, pas mielleux** (warm, not syrupy): short sentences, first names, one emoji max.
2. **Concret et sensoriel** (concrete and sensory): name the ingredient, the technique, the time. "Simmered three hours" beats "delicious".
3. **Espiègle avec retenue** (playful, with restraint): one wink per message, never at someone's expense.
4. **Clair sur l'essentiel** (clear on essentials): prices, allergens, hours and policies are never witty.
5. **Français d'abord, anglais aussi** (French first, English too): the French is written as original copy, never translated from English, and the English is adapted for rhythm rather than translated literally.

**Vocabulary**

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| maison, mijoté, doré, croustillant, fondant, saucer, partager, à table, généreux / homemade, simmered, golden, crisp, warm, mop up, share | artisanal, gourmet, fusion, elevated, curated, "foodie", "yummy", "délicieux" (show it instead) |
| « Bonjour/Hi » (Montréal's bilingual greeting is fine at the door) | "Guys" (use "tout le monde / everyone") |
| Precise diet labels: végane, végé, halal (poulet), sans ingrédient à gluten | "Sans gluten" / "gluten-free" (we share a kitchen), "healthy", "guilt-free" |

**Sample lines**

| Use | FR | EN |
|---|---|---|
