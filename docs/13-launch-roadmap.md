## 5. Top 10 risks

| # | Risk | Prevention | If it happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Permit or inspection delay** (MAPAQ, occupancy) pushes the opening | Apply at W−8; pre-inspection checklist; contractor sign-off early | Keep teaser momentum with a "pop-up" coffee cart or pre-sale gift cards; move ad spend |
| 2 | **Sign delivery late**, which blocks GBP video verification | Order at W−9; temporary vinyl banner that meets the language rule | Verify by another method offered; banner in the video |
| 3 | **Language-law non-compliance** (signage, menus, website) | FR-first everywhere; French descriptor predominant on the sign; OQLF check | Correct within the notice period; budget a reprint |
| 4 | **Kitchen overwhelmed at weekend brunch**, leading to slow tickets and bad reviews | Reservations pacing (max covers per 15 min), limited brunch menu on the busiest days, rush simulation | Pause delivery apps (1 tap), switch to 86 on the slowest dish, comp coffee for waits > 20 min |
| 5 | **Food cost overruns** (eggs, pistachio, avocado, olive oil) | Weekly price alerts on receiving; recipe costing; quarterly repricing | Swap garnish, adjust portion, reprice Plowhorses |
| 6 | **Staff shortage / turnover** | Fair scheduling two weeks ahead, tip transparency, meals, cross-training | Simplify the menu temporarily; close Monday afternoons |
| 7 | **Delivery commissions eat the margin** | +15 % delivery pricing, bundles, lowest tier after 60 days, channel shift | Remove low-margin items from apps; lean on pickup |
| 8 | **Privacy/anti-spam breach** (Law 25, CASL) | Consent records, double opt-in, privacy officer, trained staff | Incident register, notify the Commission if serious risk, fix the process |
| 9 | **Tech failure at the counter** (internet, POS) | 4G failover, POS offline mode, printed backup menu with prices, paper ticket pads | Offline mode; cash-and-card fallback; post-rush sync |
| 10 | **Slow winter launch** (December–March is Montréal's slowest period) | Cosy positioning (hot chocolate, soups), loyalty double points Tue/Wed, catering push, events | Shift ad budget to brunch bookings; mezze events; gift cards |
