# Phase 2: Visual identity & brand guidelines

## 1. Three creative directions

### Direction A: **Soleil de marché** (Market sunshine) ⭐ recommended
- **Mood:** a Mediterranean market at 10 am brought into a Montréal café. Sunlit terracotta, ripe tomato, olive oil gold and warm bread crust, on a creamy background. Joyful, generous, handmade.
- **References:** Marseille and Tunis market stalls, hand-painted shop signage, 1970s cookbook covers, Ottolenghi's use of colour, Montréal's painted Plateau staircases.
- **Colour:** terracotta tomato, bread cream, olive night, saffron, sage and a touch of faïence blue.
- **Type:** Fraunces (soft, generous serif with a wink) + Inter (clean body) + Caveat (handwritten specials).
- **Photography:** hard morning sun, strong shadows, overhead and 45° shots, hands in frame, crumbs allowed, linen and terracotta props.
- **Why:** it expresses generosity and sunshine directly, stands out on a street of black-and-white minimalist cafés, and photographs beautifully in Montréal's grey winters.

### Direction B: **Bistro parisien moderne**
- **Mood:** black-and-white tiles, brass, tomato-red awning, classic café chairs.
- **Colour:** ink black, off-white, tomato red. **Type:** Playfair Display + Work Sans.
- **Why not:** elegant, but it reads "French bistro", hides the Mediterranean kitchen and competes with dozens of Montréal bistros.

### Direction C: **Riad minimal**
- **Mood:** calm, deep teal, sand plaster, zellige tile patterns, arches.
- **Colour:** teal #1F4E5A, sand #E8D8BF, white, brass. **Type:** Cormorant + DM Sans.
- **Why not:** beautiful and calm, but more "restaurant" than "café", less energetic on social, and it leans toward one specific country.

**Recommendation: A.** It is the only direction that does all four jobs: it tells the all-day Mediterranean story in one glance, stays warm in winter, stands out on Instagram, and works across screens, cups and signage without costly finishes.

## 2. Logo system (Direction A)

### 2.1 Design brief
- **Concept:** "the smile of a good bite." The word **Miam's** in a soft, chunky Fraunces-style italic serif. Under the "M"-"i", a single **curved stroke like a smile, which is also the silhouette of a spoon**. The dot of the "i" is a small **sun**.
- **Personality:** generous (heavy weight, round terminals), playful (the italic lean, the smile), handmade (slightly irregular curves, not geometric).
- **Must work:** at 16 px (favicon), on a 1-colour stamp for bags and cups, and embroidered on aprons.
- **Legal:** the full lockup on exterior signs must include a **French descriptor that is markedly predominant**. See §7.

### 2.2 Versions

| Version | Composition | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary lockup** | `Miam's` wordmark + smile-spoon + "RESTO CAFÉ" in spaced Inter SemiBold small caps below | Signage, menu covers, website header |
| **Stacked / secondary** | `Miam's` above; "RESTO · CAFÉ" and "cuisine méditerranéenne" on two lines below | Square spaces, cups, stickers, social avatar backdrop |
| **Icon / monogram** | Rounded "M" with the smile-spoon under it inside a terracotta sun disc | Favicon, app icon, social avatar, stamps, latte-art stencil |
| **Wordmark only** | `Miam's` + smile | Small inline uses, merch, email footers |

**Clear space:** the height of the "M" cap on all sides. **Minimum size:** primary 30 mm / 120 px wide; icon 8 mm / 24 px (16 px favicon uses the simplified icon without the sun rays).
**Colour versions:** terracotta on cream (default), cream on terracotta, olive night on cream, 1-colour black, 1-colour white.
**Don'ts:** no stretching, outlines, drop shadows or gradients; don't place it on busy photos without a cream badge; don't recolour the sun separately.

### 2.3 Ready-to-use prompts

**For a designer (Illustrator/Figma):**
> Create a vector wordmark for "Miam's", a Mediterranean all-day café in Montréal. Base: Fraunces 9pt Soft, Black Italic (SOFT 100, WONK 1) as a starting point, then hand-refine: open counters, rounded terminals, a slightly bouncy baseline. Replace the tittle of the "i" with a small sun (circle + 8 short rounded rays). Draw one smile-shaped curved stroke under "Mi", with a slightly thicker right end so it reads as a spoon. Deliver primary, stacked, icon (M + smile in a sun disc) and wordmark versions in terracotta #B8472A on cream #F6EEDF, plus 1-colour. Formats: AI, SVG, PDF, PNG @1×/2×/4×, favicon ICO/SVG.

**For an AI image tool (concept exploration only; always redraw the final in vector):**
> Logo concept, flat vector, wordmark "Miam's" in a warm chunky italic serif, a small sun as the dot of the i, a smile-shaped spoon curve under the letters, terracotta (#B8472A) on cream (#F6EEDF) background, Mediterranean café, friendly, handmade feeling, minimal, no gradients, no mockup, centered, lots of empty space

> Icon concept, flat vector, rounded letter M with a small smiling spoon curve beneath, inside a terracotta circle with subtle sun rays, cream background, simple, works at 16 pixels, no text other than M

## 5. Graphic elements
- **Sun-tile pattern:** a 4-petal faïence tile (inspired by Mediterranean cement tiles) redrawn as a simple line motif in Terracotta or Sauge on Crème at 8 % opacity. Used for backgrounds, bags, napkins and web section dividers.
- **Smile-spoon stroke:** a standalone underline element to highlight words ("fait *maison*").
- **Icons (line, 2 px stroke, round caps):** coffee cup, croissant, pan, bowl, leaf (végé), sprout (végane), halal shown as a text badge ("Halal"), not a religious symbol, chili (relevé), allergen letters in circles (G L Œ N A S So P C M Su).
- **Illustration style:** single-weight hand-drawn line illustrations of ingredients (lemon, olive branch, chickpea, cardamom pod, pistachio), used sparingly as stickers.
- **Textures:** subtle paper grain on print only; never on screens.
- **Stickers (die-cut, 5 designs):** "Ici, on dit miam." · "Fait maison, fait miam." · sun icon · "Pistache club" · "Brunch sans file / No-queue brunch".

## 7. Brand applications

> ⚠️ **Language-law rule for Québec signage:** with "Miam's" as the brand, exterior signs must also show a **French generic term that is markedly predominant** (under the Charter of the French Language as amended by Bill 96, with signage rules in force since June 2025, where a trademark is not in French). Recommended exterior sign: **"Miam's"** logo + **"RESTO CAFÉ · CUISINE MÉDITERRANÉENNE"** set large enough to be markedly predominant (in practice, the French text needs to occupy at least twice the space of the trademark text; **verify the exact current criteria with the OQLF or a lawyer before ordering the sign**). ⚠️ OWNER ACTION: file the "Miam's" trademark with CIPO (≈ $450+ government fee; verify).

## 8. Brand guidelines document (brand book outline)

1. Welcome: who we are (Phase 1 short story, promise, values)
2. Voice: principles, vocabulary, sample lines FR/EN
3. Logo: versions, construction, clear space, minimum sizes, colour versions, misuse
4. Colour: palette with values, ratios, accessible pairings, don'ts
5. Typography: fonts, hierarchy for web/print/screens, licensing
6. Graphic elements: pattern, stroke, icons, illustrations, stickers
7. Photography & video: lighting, angles, props, grading preset, do/don't examples
8. Menu design rules: badges, allergen icons, price style (Phase 3)
9. Digital: website components, screen templates, social templates
10. Applications: signage (with language rule), packaging, uniforms, stationery
11. Accessibility: contrast, minimum sizes, alt text rules
12. Files and contacts: where the masters live, who approves
